# Impeccable — gideonb.me

## Design Context

### Users

Two readers, in this order.

1. **A hiring panel for VP / Head of Design roles in London.** Miro asks for a case presentation at stage three, Proton runs a portfolio review, IDEO wants work examples in a challenge / role / approach / impact format at application stage. These people read properly, in a meeting, often on a shared screen. They are looking for evidence that this person has run a function, not decorated one.
2. **A design leader giving it ninety seconds** before deciding whether to book the call. They will scroll, not read. They must be able to take the whole argument from headings, numbers and diagrams alone.

Secondary: recruiters scanning for keywords and seniority signals, and people arriving from an article who want to know who wrote it.

The job to be done, on both sides: decide quickly whether Gideon Bullock has built a design organisation before, and whether he can do it again here.

### Brand Personality

**Quiet, exacting, unimpressed.**

British English. Never em dashes: commas, colons, semicolons or brackets. No emoji. Direct and unshowy. Precise about authorship, including when the answer is "somebody else drew this". Confident without performance; the writing does not sell, it states. It should never sound like a CV, and it should never sound like marketing.

The emotional target is **credibility**, not delight. A reader should finish a section thinking "that is a person who has actually done this", not "that is a nice website".

### Aesthetic Direction

**Editorial, structural, quiet.** Warm off-white paper, near-black ink, one grotesque, generous measure. The character comes from structure and typography, never from ornament.

- **Theme: light**, warm. The site is read in daylight, on a laptop, in a hiring context. Dark full-bleed bands are used as *punctuation* inside the light page, not as a theme.
- **Palette:** paper `oklch(0.968 0.006 85)` (`#f8f6f2`), ink `oklch(0.229 0.008 65)` (`#1e1a16`), warm neutrals through `#5c5347` / `#8a7d6e` / `#b5a08a`, rules `#e8e2da`. Neutrals are already tinted toward the brand hue (~78). Two matched accents at equal lightness and chroma: amber `oklch(0.575 0.075 62)` for *my call*, teal `oklch(0.575 0.075 195)` for *the team's work*. Dark bands on `oklch(0.185 0.010 70)`.
- **Type:** one family, **Schibsted Grotesk**. A Norwegian newspaper face built for headlines and small text: tighter apertures and real presence at 700 and 800, still completely quiet at 400. Replaces Inter site-wide. No serif. No second family.
- **References:** an exhibition catalogue, a standards document, a well-set annual report. Newspaper masthead discipline for the numbers.
- **Anti-references:** a blog post with a big header image. A CV rendered as HTML. Agency-portfolio maximalism. Anything that looks templated, and anything that looks generated.

### Design Principles

1. **Structure is the evidence.** The arguments here are organisational, so the proof is diagrams of structure, not screenshots of screens. Where a diagram can carry a claim, it does; where it cannot, prose does. Never both.
2. **Authorship is visible at a glance.** Every block says who made the call, through marker form, block label and ground colour. "I created the conditions and other people did the work" is the larger claim and gets the larger treatment. Colour never carries this alone.
3. **The ninety-second read must work.** Landmarks the eye can find while scrolling, headings that carry the argument rather than naming a section, and a takeaway line under every diagram.
4. **Say less, and hold the line on what is said.** Some sentences are load-bearing and survive every layout pass. Everything else can go. No filler, no padding, no numbers that are not doing work.
5. **Nothing dressed up.** No ornament, no gradient, no glass, no side-stripe accents, no card grids, no imagery that flatters rather than evidences. Confidential figures stay out even when they would help.

### Constraints

- Astro 7, static output, no client framework. Hand-written CSS in `src/styles/global.css`.
- Page copy lives in markdown at the repo root (`work.md`, `homepage.md`), parsed at build time by `src/lib/page.mjs` using `::: section` markers, so it stays editable in Obsidian. Block labels are data, set in each case's `-meta` block.
- The ten article slugs are frozen. `Gideons_Vault/` is never written to. `.migration/baseline/` is never regenerated.
- **Confidentiality (Toyota):** out — margin contribution, revenue share by programme, design-system savings and build-cost comparison, the 300+ properties figure, DesignOps tooling saving, support-call volumes, subscription non-renewal rates, screen counts, service blueprints, unreleased V3 interface work, colleague names. In — 0 to 40+, £10m+ annual internal revenue, highest retention in the company, all App Store rating data, research counts, SUS scores, complaint-category timeline.
- Accessibility: contrast held at WCAG AA for body text, meaning never carried by colour alone, motion limited to one orchestrated reveal and honouring `prefers-reduced-motion`.
