Case one · the sequence

2018  →  2026

How the capability grew

Five capabilities in the order they were established.

First

UI design

Screens for flows that business analysts and product managers had already written, in support of Connected Technologies.

Earned → a place inside the product teams.

Second

UX design

When the app passed into Connected Technologies’ ownership, the flows came with it.

Earned → the flows themselves.

Third

UX research and analytics

The same people were wireframing without knowing what users did.

Earned → evidence, and with it the right to frame the problem.

Fourth

Product design

The team moved closer to the core business.

Earned → a close enough view of the business to see what it could not see.

The question changed

Fifth

Service design

Connected Technologies owned the app in full and had to work with the rest of Toyota in Europe. Nothing like it existed anywhere in Toyota.

Earned → the joins between business units that nobody owned.

Scope: one screen

Scope: the business behind it

Left to right is seven years. The band beneath is how much of the customer’s experience design was allowed to own, from one screen to connected vehicle apps, in-vehicle multimedia and EV.
