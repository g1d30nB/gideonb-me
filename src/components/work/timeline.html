Case two · the outcome

Internal customer-experience reporting, Jul 2024 to Aug 2025

Thirteen months from complaint driver to differentiator

Design dominated the complaint list when the repurposed app landed. Then it left the list altogether.

2024

Design dominates the complaints

The deprecated US app lands in Europe at 1.9 stars. Navigation customers cannot learn, information displays they cannot read, no dark mode, driving analytics nobody understands.

Spring 2025

The language shifts

“Intuitive”, “simple and effective”, “very well thought out” start appearing in customer feedback where the complaints used to be.

June 2025

Design credited by name

An internal customer-experience report attributes reduced complaint volume directly to intuitive design. The first time on record that design was named as the cause of an improvement.

August 2025

Out of the top five

Displaced by backend and connectivity issues outside design’s ownership. The 4.6 star rating stabilises.
